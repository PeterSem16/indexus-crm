The approved Variant A must be implemented literally: its explicit light surfaces, red brand treatment, blue Mission accents, green queue accents, violet Back Office treatment, gradient header, one-third summary/two-thirds selection workspace, two-column cards, and horizontal footer CTA structure. Mission and inbound cards use independent expanders for real operating details such as hours and phone numbers; expanding must not change selection.

**Why:** A generic theme-token restyle with the old layout and a larger Start Shift button was rejected as visibly different from the approved proposal.

**How to apply:** Compare production directly with the approved Variant A component. Preserve real dashboard behavior and mobile support, but do not substitute the existing modal's theme styling or structural placement for the approved design. Keep the modal dense enough to scan several cards while allowing expanded operational details.
